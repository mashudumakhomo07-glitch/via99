Terms & conditions

Via99 Driver Agreement

By registering as a Via99 driver, you agree to the following:

1. Independent contractor status. You operate as an independent contractor, not an employee of Via99. You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and vehicle maintenance.

2. Vehicle standards. Your vehicle must remain roadworthy, insured, and compliant with South African traffic legislation at all times. Via99 reserves the right to suspend your account if your vehicle fails an inspection.

3. Professional conduct. You must maintain a rating of 3.5 or above. Abusive, discriminatory, or unsafe behaviour will result in immediate account suspension.

4. Commission structure. Via99 takes a 5% service fee from each trip fare. This is deducted automatically before payout. The remaining 80% is your earnings; 15% covers operational costs.

5. Acceptance rate. Consistently rejecting trips without valid reason may affect your account standing and priority in trip matching.

6. Data and privacy. Your personal information is processed in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Documents submitted during onboarding are used solely for compliance verification.

7. Account suspension. Via99 reserves the right to suspend or terminate any driver account that violates these terms, receives sustained low ratings, or engages in fraudulent activity.

8. Pending approval. Your account will be reviewed within 2–3 business days. You may not accept trips until your account is approved.
